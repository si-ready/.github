# SI Ready

Ready to use. Not to play with.

SuperInstance is the playground — hundreds of repos, experiments, seeds, dead ends, compost. That's where things are born.

This is where they graduate to. Applications, toolkits, and SDKs that are ready for someone to actually *use*. Not to explore the idea. To do the job.

## What belongs here

- It works. Someone besides the builder has run it.
- It's documented. A stranger can figure it out in 10 minutes.
- It's stable. Not changing weekly. The API won't break tomorrow.
- It solves a real problem. Not a demo. Not a proof of concept.

## What doesn't

- Experiments, seeds, playgrounds → SuperInstance
- Ideas, essays, patterns → SuperInstance or AI-Writings
- Dead ends → they stay where they died, as compost

## The bar

If you have to explain why it's ready, it's not ready. The work should speak.

Few repos. Only the gold.
